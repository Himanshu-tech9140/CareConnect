# CareConnect design brief

## Direction exploration

### 1. Clinical Calm — 0.04
A quiet, high-trust clinical operations interface with deep navy structure, cool mint accents, warm-white surfaces, and crisp data hierarchy.

### 2. Signal Room — 0.07
A high-contrast command-center dashboard with graphite surfaces, electric blue signal colors, and denser monitoring panels.

### 3. Human Thread — 0.03
A warmer care coordination product with soft sand surfaces, coral accents, and conversational timeline-led layouts.

## Committed direction: Clinical Calm

CareConnect uses a calm, precise visual language for clinical work: dark navy navigation, warm-white canvas, pale blue-gray surfaces, mint/teal primary actions, and coral reserved for attention states. The interface favors strong hierarchy over decoration, with patient identity and clinical status always visible in context.

- **Design movement:** modern clinical operations, with editorial restraint and high information density.
- **Core principles:** calm before clever, patient context stays visible, status is scannable, one clear next action per panel, and accessibility is part of the clinical workflow.
- **Color philosophy:** navy anchors navigation and trust; teal marks active workflow and safe progress; blue-gray supports secondary content; amber and coral signal attention without becoming alarming.
- **Layout paradigm:** a persistent left rail, compact utility header, 12-column content canvas, and responsive single-column fallback below tablet width.
- **Signature elements:** patient identity blocks with MRN and care-team context, rounded status pills, subtle clinical timeline connectors, and a quiet “today” marker on dashboards.
- **Interaction philosophy:** immediate visual feedback, explicit confirmation for clinical state changes, keyboard-visible focus, and no hidden critical actions.
- **Animation:** short 150–200ms transitions for panels, route entry, and status updates; no decorative motion that competes with patient data.
- **Typography system:** Inter for operational UI, using 14–16px body text, 12px metadata, 28–36px page headings, and weight rather than oversized type for emphasis.
- **Brand essence:** dependable, human, connected care.
- **Brand voice:** direct, reassuring, and clinically literate without being cold.
- **Wordmark/logo:** a compact cross-like care mark nested inside a rounded square, using negative space to suggest a connected patient-provider link. The same mark is used in the sidebar, favicon, and project icon.
- **Signature brand color:** `#42D5B2` mint teal.
